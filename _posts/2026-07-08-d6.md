Clear every **task tile** — they show a required face, `TOP` wanting that value
on the die's top face and `BTM` on the bottom — to light the **goal**, then roll
onto it. Along the way there are conveyors that slide the die a cell without
turning it, spin tiles that rotate it in place, and paired portals.

The build above is vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step. The rules
live in a DOM-free core, kept portable so the same logic carries over to the
Godot port — the renderer is the half that gets thrown away.
